Abstract
Small and medium enterprises face significant challenges with limited
marketing budgets and expertise. AdPulseAl addresses this gap through
Generative Al automation, transforming product information into ready-
to-publish campaigns across multiple platforms.
The platform extends PMI (Personalized Messaging Infrastructure
beyond SMS-only operations to an omnichannel content generation
system. It incorporates role-based governance with usage telemetry for
safer, more controlled platform operations.
